Trend rays follow the top-down method (1M → 15m): the first down ray starts at the highest high, the first up ray at the lowest low, price may not cut through a ray, and each new ray starts at the previous one's last touch. Thick rays belong to the selected timeframe. "At line" = possible bounce entry, "Broken" = a candle closed through it (exit).

Order blocks (blue = bullish demand, purple = bearish supply) are the last opposite candle before the move that broke structure; "fresh" = price hasn't come back yet, "tested" = it has. A zone is removed once a candle closes through it.

How to read the rest: swing highs/lows are confirmed pivots. BOS (break of structure) = a candle closes beyond the last swing in the trend's direction — the trend continues (uptrend: close above the last swing high; downtrend: close below the last swing low). CHoCH (change of character) = the first close beyond a swing against the trend — an early reversal warning. Dashed lines are the levels that would print the next break. Higher-timeframe structure is stronger; a BOS that agrees with the 4H/1D bias is the higher-probability one.

Fibonacci is drawn on the leg that printed the last break: after a bullish BOS, buyers look for price to pull back into discount (below 50%), ideally the OTE zone 61.8–78.6%; after a bearish BOS, sellers look for premium. Liquidity: stops rest above old highs (buy-side, blue) and below old lows (sell-side, pink) — equal highs/lows are the strongest pools. A sweep (yellow) is a wick through a level that closes back inside, often before a move the other way. Yellow volume bars = 2× normal volume. POC = price with the most volume in the last 200 candles. FVG = an imbalance gap that price often returns to fill. Not financial advice.

Trend following systems. They trade 4H or daily candles, hold for days to weeks and use wide stops that follow price, so fees are small compared with the risk. Donchian breakout: buy when a candle closes above the highest high of the last 20 candles; first stop 2×ATR below; exit when price breaks the lowest low of the last 10 candles. EMA trend: buy when price closes above the 50 EMA while the 50 EMA is above the 200 EMA; the stop trails 3×ATR below the highest high since entry. Structure trend: buy on a BOS/CHoCH up while the daily structure is bullish; stop under the last swing low, raised to each new higher low; exit after a bearish CHoCH/BOS. Short trades are the mirror image (choose "Long + short"). Entries fill at the next candle's open, stops at the stop price (or the open if price gaps through it). Funding is charged on every open position as a cost, which is conservative. Trend systems usually win only 30–45% of trades and earn their money from a few big winners — expect long losing streaks.
How the test works. Candles are replayed one by one and each decision only uses what was known at that moment. A trade is entered on the next candle after its signal (BOS + OTE uses a limit order at 70.5% of the BOS leg). The stop and a fixed target (1.5R, 2R or 3R) are set at entry; if one candle touches both, the stop counts first. Trades still open after 24 hours (15m), 2 days (1h) or 5 days (4h) are closed at market. Fees of 0.05% per side are taken off every trade. Ray bounce needs 4H structure in the same direction and a candle that touches the ray and closes back on the right side. BOS + OTE needs a BOS that agrees with 4H structure. Sweep reversal needs a wick through the previous day's high or low that closes back inside, then a CHoCH/BOS the other way within 12 candles. Precision Entry is the "Precision Entry Signal" TradingView indicator: EMA 21 crosses EMA 55, price on the right side of EMA 200, RSI ≥ 55 (≤ 45 for shorts), volume above its 20-candle average and a close beyond the last 5/5 pivot from the past 50 candles; stop 1.5×ATR, target as chosen (the indicator's default is 2R). "Keep" = at least 30 trades, more than +0.1R per trade and profit factor above 1.2. Gold before December 2025 is tested on PAXG, a Binance token backed by physical gold that follows the gold price closely. "Avg daily range" shows how volatile the tested period was, so you can compare calm and wild markets. Past results don't guarantee future results; the trend rays here are drawn on the entry timeframe only, so they differ a little from the top-down rays on the Analysis tab. Not financial advice.
